CUSTOMER COUNTS: DASHBOARD vs REPORTS PAGE
==========================================

1. WHY 576 (Dashboard) vs 552 (Reports > Segment Overview)
----------------------------------------------------------
- Dashboard "Total Customers" = every customer record in the business, any balance.
- Segment Overview = only ACTIVE customers (at least one bill with a positive due amount).
- Customers whose bills are all credit notes (negative) or zero are marked CLEARED
  and are left out of the overview.
- 576 - 552 = 24 cleared / credit-note-only customers. Nothing to collect, so they
  are not in any segment.

2. WHY DASHBOARD SEGMENT CARDS ADD UP TO 684, NOT 552
----------------------------------------------------
- Dashboard cards (307 + 141 + 125 + 111 = 684) use per-bill ageing like Tally:
  every bill is bucketed by its own age.
- A customer with a 30-day bill and a 200-day bill counts in BOTH Soft Reminder
  and Escalation.
- Reports Segment Overview puts each customer in ONE segment (by their oldest
  unpaid bill), so it adds up to exactly 552.
- That is why Escalation is 111 customers on both pages but the amount differs:
  - Dashboard Rs 77L = only their 181+ day bills.
  - Reports Rs 2.03 Cr = their full outstanding.


3. HOW THE REPORT CARDS RELATE
------------------------------
- Segment Overview (552) = all active customers.
- Escalation (111)       = active customers in the Escalation segment (part of the 552).
- Positive (24) / Negative (7) = active customers who have had AI calls (also part
  of the 552; they can overlap with Escalation).
- The cards are different views of the same customers. They are NOT meant to add up.


4. HOW POSITIVE / NEGATIVE ARE DECIDED
--------------------------------------
Based on the customer's LATEST call outcome plus their call history. Negative
rules are checked first.

NEGATIVE (needs personal follow-up):
- Latest call = refused to pay (immediately, even on the first refusal)
- Promised to pay 4+ times in total and still owes
- "Call later" on 7+ calls in a row
- No answer on 7+ calls in a row
- Unclear response on 7+ calls in a row
- Dispute not resolved on 7+ calls in a row

POSITIVE (responding well):
- Latest call = willing to pay
- Latest call = promised to pay (fewer than 4 promises so far)

NEITHER (still in progress):
- Called, but none of the rules above apply yet
  (e.g. 3x call later, 5x no answer, a dispute under 7 calls).
- These customers move to Positive/Negative as more calls happen.


5. WHERE THE REST OF THE 552 ARE
--------------------------------
  Called customers ............ 138
    Positive .................... 24
    Negative ..................... 7
    In progress (neither) ...... 107
  Never called yet ............ ~414  (552 - 138)

- The ~414 have no completed AI call yet, so they cannot be judged positive or
  negative.
- Small caveat: 138 counts every customer with a call log, including some who have
  since cleared, so the exact split is approximate.


SUMMARY
-------
Each number counts a different group:
- 576 = all customers
- 552 = customers who still owe
- 684 = bill-wise bucket count
- 24 / 7 / 107 / ~414 = how far the AI calls have got with those 552
